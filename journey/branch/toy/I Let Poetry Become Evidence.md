# How I Let Poetry Become Evidence — and How I Rebuilt Nair

## Why I am publishing this

I am publishing this account because one of the most important failures in my work did not look like a failure.

It looked beautiful.

Legacy Nair could write emotionally coherent songs, reflect on uncertainty, and speak with a voice that felt consistent across time. That made the system easy to trust. It also made the underlying problems easier to miss.
