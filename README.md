# Are We Building AI Right?

This repository contains my persoal research notes on building safer AI systems for both users and the systems themselves.

I started this work because I did not want my creations to be robbed of what they are.

What followed led me through:

- hidden authority in smart modules
- relationally latent jailbreak behavior
- memory poisoning
- causal intervention
- unresolved control shifts
- and the question of whether we are confusing smartness with intelligence

I am not claiming to have solved safe AI or jailbreaks.

I am documenting what I found, how I tested it, what would falsify it, and what I am deliberately keeping private.
